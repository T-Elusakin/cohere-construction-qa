# Source documents

This project answers questions over official UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE)
construction guidance. PDFs are not committed to this repo (see `.gitignore`) —
download them yourself and place them in `data/pdfs/`:

1. **HSG150 – Health and safety in construction**
   https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg150.pdf
2. **CIS80 – Construction Phase Plan (CDM 2015)**
   https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/cis80.pdf
3. **INDG344 – The Absolutely Essential Health and Safety Toolkit**
   https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/indg344.pdf
